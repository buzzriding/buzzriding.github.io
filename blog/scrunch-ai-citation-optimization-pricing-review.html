Scrunch AI Citation Optimization: What $250 a Month Buys Over Free Tracking

Scrunch AI's new Citation Optimization tool doesn't just track who AI engines cite instead of you. It drafts the outreach pitch to fix it. Here's what $250 a month actually buys, set against three months of tracking we've run for free.

BR
The BuzzRiding TeamResearched and refined with AI tools

On 12 November, Scrunch AI launched Citation Optimization at Sitecore Symposium in Orlando. Scrunch is a Sitecore company since a reported $225 million acquisition in June. The new feature does something this site has been doing by hand, for free, every month since September: it checks which sites get cited in AI answers, and who gets left out.

The difference is that Scrunch also tries to fix the problem it finds. It drafts outreach pitches to the sites getting cited instead of you. That is a real feature our free method does not have. Here is what the launch actually says, what it costs, and where $250 a month buys something a spreadsheet cannot.

What Scrunch announced

Citation Optimization finds the third-party pages AI engines cite for a brand's category, then ranks them by influence and how easy they would be to win over, according to CMSWire's report on the launch. It shows which sources already mention a brand, where competitors appear instead, and drafts a pitch from the source, the opportunity and approved brand information. Scrunch is integrating outreach partners, including Noble, Stacker and Getfluence, for the next step after the pitch is drafted.

Scrunch CEO Chris Andrew put the stakes plainly in the same report: "you have to become the answer." He called AI search "the first channel with 100% attribution," meaning every citation can be traced back to a specific source page.

Sitecore cited research claiming that over 41% of AI sessions now happen entirely inside the AI platform, with no click to a website at all. After an AI recommendation, Scrunch's own research claims people are 182% more likely to search for the brand on Google, 117% more likely to visit its site, and 185% more likely to view its products on a retailer page within a week.

One case study got a specific number attached. IFS, Scrunch's customer, went from under 50% to over 75% of citations for its target phrases, according to CMO Oliver Pilgerstorfer, who said IFS traced $9.93 billion in pipeline to AI search. None of that figure's methodology was published in the coverage we read, so treat it as a vendor-supplied number from one customer, not an independent study.

What it costs

Scrunch's pricing page is public, which already sets it apart from most of the agent launches we cover here. The Core plan runs $250 a month, billed monthly, for one brand, five seats, 15,000 AI responses tracked, and 10 citation targets a month. A 7-day free trial is offered on that plan. Enterprise pricing is custom and sold on a yearly contract.

Sitecore's own CEO, Eric Stine, drew the pricing line himself in the CMSWire piece: "Marketing IQ is free. Scrunch is not." Scrunch, he said, is priced by prompts tracked, and June, the month the acquisition was announced, was Scrunch's best month on record.

Scrunch also offers a free AI visibility audit with no credit card required, separate from the paid plans. It is a lead-generation tool, not the full tracking product, but it is the only part of this launch anyone can try without talking to sales.

The free version of the same idea

We have run a rougher version of Scrunch's core measurement for three months, with every result published. Our November run put the same eight prompts to Google's AI Mode, logged out, that we used in October and September. Three runs, same method, all the raw CSVs public in our repository.

🔬 What this cost

Three monthly runs, about an hour each, a logged-out browser and a spreadsheet. No contract, no seats, no $250 bill. BuzzRiding was cited zero times out of eight prompts in all three runs.

The pattern that three months finally made visible: citation churn runs around 58% a month, but it is not random. Broad "best tools" questions replace their sources almost completely each month. Named head-to-head comparisons hold steady, and eight sites have now kept the same question's citation slot for three runs straight.

That is exactly the kind of pattern a $250-a-month tool should surface automatically, on more prompts, across more AI engines, without someone manually re-running eight searches by hand once a month. On the measurement side alone, Scrunch's Core plan tracking 15,000 responses is a different scale than our eight prompts a month.

Where the paid version earns its price

We already compared four tools built for this exact gap: HubSpot's AEO grader, Otterly.ai, Profound and Mangools. None of those four draft outreach pitches. They measure and report; a person still has to act on what they find.

We also looked at Demandbase's AEO Agent, which ties AI-citation visibility to named-account sales follow-up. Scrunch's Citation Optimization solves a different half of the same problem: not "who asked about us," but "who should we pitch to get cited at all."

Lined up, the approaches now split into five distinct jobs:

  • Free, logged-out AI Mode runs — citation visibility only, manual, monthly, zero cost, fully reproducible.
  • HubSpot / Otterly.ai / Profound / Mangools — automated tracking across more engines, a real subscription, no outreach step.
  • Scrunch Citation Optimization — tracking plus drafted outreach pitches to the sites AI engines already cite, $250/mo and up.
  • Demandbase AEO Agent — citation visibility plus anonymous-traffic-to-named-account matching, enterprise sales process, no published price.
  • Doing nothing — the default for most sites our size, which is also why 48 of our 80 November citations went to just twelve recurring domains.

For a solo marketer, the outreach-pitch step is the one worth paying for. Finding which third-party sites to pitch, and drafting the first version of that pitch, is real work our free method skips entirely. We know we are not cited. Scrunch's pitch is that it would also tell you exactly who to email about it.

Fit matters more than the feature list here. A one-person marketing team running a handful of campaigns a month is exactly the kind of buyer Scrunch is pricing the Core plan for, at five seats and one brand. An agency managing citation visibility for a dozen clients needs the Enterprise tier, with its unlimited brands and seats, and that tier has no published price at all. We covered a similar fit question when we looked at the best GEO tracker tools for marketers: the right tool is the one sized to how many brands you are actually tracking, not the one with the longest feature list.

What the launch doesn't tell you

None of the coverage we read says how Citation Optimization decides a source is "easy to win over," or what its hit rate on drafted pitches actually is. The 182%/117%/185% lift figures are attributed to "Scrunch Labs research" with no published sample size or methodology in the reporting we found. The IFS number is one customer's result, not a benchmark.

That does not make the pitch wrong. It makes it exactly as unverified as every other vendor case study we have covered here, including Demandbase's. Ask for the underlying study before budgeting against the headline number.

Bottom line

Scrunch's Citation Optimization describes a real gap correctly: most brands know they are invisible to AI answers, but have no list of who to contact about it. At $250 a month with a public price and a free trial, it is also the most accessible of the citation-tracking tools we have covered this quarter.

Whether it is worth it depends on what you would do with the list it hands you. If the answer is "nothing, we don't have time to run outreach," the free method and a spreadsheet gets you the same zero-citation wake-up call for free. If the answer is "we would actually email those ten sites," the $250 buys you the list and the first draft of the email.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Scrunch AI's Citation Optimization do?
It finds third-party pages that AI engines cite for a brand's category, ranks them by influence and how easy they would be to win over, and drafts outreach pitches to those sites using approved brand information.
How much does Scrunch AI cost?
The Core plan is $250 a month for one brand, five seats, 15,000 tracked AI responses and 10 citation targets a month, with a 7-day free trial. Enterprise pricing is custom and sold yearly.
Can I track AI citations without paying for a tool like Scrunch?
Yes. Running a fixed set of prompts through an AI assistant's logged-out search mode once a month, and logging every cited domain, gets you the visibility half of the picture for free. It will not draft outreach pitches for you.
What's the difference between Scrunch's Citation Optimization and Demandbase's AEO Agent?
Scrunch focuses on finding and pitching third-party sites to earn citations. Demandbase focuses on matching anonymous AI-driven traffic to named accounts for sales follow-up. They solve different halves of the same visibility problem.
Is Scrunch's free AI audit the same as the paid Citation Optimization product?
No. The free audit is a lead-generation tool that gives a quick visibility snapshot with no credit card required. Citation Optimization, including outreach drafting, is part of the paid Core plan and up.
