5 Best GEO Tracker Tools for Marketers in 2026 (And Who Actually Needs One)

Almost every GEO tracker roundup is written by a company selling one. This one isn't. Here are five generative engine optimization trackers compared by price and use case — plus the honest answer on whether you need one at all.

What a GEO tracker actually does

A GEO tracker monitors whether AI engines mention or cite your brand. It runs prompts through ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews on a schedule. Then it reports your visibility, your position in answers, and your share of voice against competitors.

If GEO is new to you, start with our plain-English guide: GEO Explained: What Marketers Need to Know About AI Search in 2026. This article assumes you know why AI citations matter and want to measure them.

The core capabilities to compare are simple. Citation frequency: how often AI mentions you. Share of voice: how you stack up against competitors. Source tracking: which of your pages AI engines actually pull from. Prompt coverage: how many questions the tool monitors, and across how many AI platforms.

🔎 The Honest Filter

GEO trackers range from free to $1,000+ per month. Most marketers reading vendor comparison pages end up over-buying. Match the tool to your publishing volume and your boss's reporting expectations — not to the feature list.

1. Mangools AI Search Grader — best free starting point

Mangools AI Search GraderFree

A free tool that grades your brand's visibility across AI engines, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Mistral. You enter your brand and get a snapshot of how AI models see you.

Where it falls short: It's a snapshot, not monitoring. No scheduled tracking, no competitor share of voice, no alerts. Think diagnostic, not dashboard.

Works well

  • Free — zero commitment
  • Covers multiple AI engines
  • Fast first read on your AI visibility
  • Good for one-off client audits

Watch out for

  • No ongoing monitoring
  • No competitor benchmarking
  • Limited depth per report
  • You'll outgrow it if GEO becomes a KPI

2. Otterly AI — best budget monitoring

Otterly AIFrom $29/month (Lite)

Otterly is the most credible budget option we found. The Lite plan tracks 15 search prompts for $29 per month. It monitors brand mentions, link citations, and sentiment across major AI platforms. The Standard plan jumps to 100 prompts at $189 per month.

Where it falls short: 15 prompts is tight. You'll spend real time choosing which questions matter most. Deeper analytics sit behind the higher tiers.

Works well

  • Real monitoring at $29/month
  • Simple, non-technical interface
  • Tracks citations and sentiment
  • Easy step up as needs grow

Watch out for

  • 15 prompts on the entry plan
  • Big price jump to the next tier
  • Less advanced analytics than rivals

3. HubSpot AEO — easiest for HubSpot shops

HubSpot AEOFree 28 days, then $50/month

HubSpot's answer engine optimization tool tracks 25 prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. It's free for the first 28 days, then $50 per month. If your company already runs on HubSpot, this is the lowest-friction option to get AI visibility into existing reports.

Where it falls short: It's newer than the specialists and the prompt cap is fixed. Teams outside the HubSpot ecosystem get less value from the integration story.

Works well

  • 28-day free trial, no sales call
  • 25 prompts for $50/month
  • Native fit with HubSpot reporting
  • Simple enough to demo to a boss

Watch out for

  • Fixed prompt cap
  • Fewer engines than specialists
  • Young product, still maturing

4. Peec AI — best mid-tier for competitive tracking

Peec AIFrom €89/month

Peec AI is built for marketing teams that need proper competitive benchmarking. It tracks brand performance across six AI engines with prompt organization, brand ranking, and source identification. Analytics are noticeably deeper than the budget tier.

Where it falls short: At roughly $95 per month, it needs a business case. Solo marketers and small blogs rarely have the citation volume to justify it yet.

Works well

  • Six AI engines, no add-ons
  • Strong competitor benchmarking
  • Source identification is genuinely useful
  • Built for team workflows

Watch out for

  • ~$95/month entry point
  • Overkill below ~10k monthly visits
  • Euro pricing adds exchange friction

5. Profound — the enterprise heavyweight

ProfoundEnterprise pricing

Profound is the market leader in AI search intelligence and raised a $96M Series C in 2026. Large brands use it for deep AI visibility tracking at serious scale. If you work in enterprise marketing, this is the name your leadership has already heard.

Where it falls short: Pricing is enterprise-level and opaque. Monthly costs commonly run well past $500. This is a procurement decision, not a credit-card signup.

Works well

  • Deepest tracking on the market
  • Strong momentum and funding
  • Enterprise-grade reporting
  • Handles huge prompt volumes

Watch out for

  • Enterprise pricing only
  • Massive overkill for most readers
  • Long sales and onboarding cycle

💡 Our Recommendation

Do this in order. Run Mangools free to get a baseline. If AI answers matter to your traffic, start Otterly at $29 or HubSpot's 28-day trial. Graduate to Peec when a competitor starts beating you in AI answers. Ignore Profound unless procurement is involved.

The free alternative: track GEO manually

Here's what the vendor listicles won't say. At low traffic volumes, you can track GEO with zero tools. We did exactly this in our four-week GEO experiment — manual prompt testing found real citation changes without a subscription.

The manual workflow takes about 30 minutes a week. Write 10–12 prompts your customers would actually ask. Run each through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews monthly. Record mentions and cited URLs in a spreadsheet. Watch Google Search Console for AI-driven query patterns.

This mirrors the approach in our free AI competitor content audit workflow. Free works until your prompt list outgrows a spreadsheet. That's the honest trigger point for paying.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GEO tracker?
A GEO (generative engine optimization) tracker is software that monitors how often AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini mention or cite your brand. It runs a set of prompts on a schedule and reports visibility, citations, and share of voice against competitors.
Do I actually need a GEO tracker in 2026?
Only if AI answers are a meaningful discovery channel for you. If your site gets under a few thousand monthly visits, manual monthly prompt testing is usually enough. Once stakeholders ask for AI visibility reporting, or a competitor dominates AI answers in your niche, a paid tracker earns its cost.
What's the cheapest way to start tracking GEO?
Free: run Mangools AI Search Grader for a baseline, then test 10–12 customer prompts manually each month. Cheapest paid: Otterly AI's Lite plan at $29/month for 15 tracked prompts, or HubSpot AEO's 28-day free trial with 25 prompts.
How is GEO tracking different from rank tracking?
Rank trackers measure your position in a list of links. GEO trackers measure whether you appear inside a synthesized answer. AI engines don't show ten blue links — they name a handful of sources. You're tracking inclusion and citation, not position.
Which AI engines should a GEO tracker cover?
At minimum: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini. Those drive most AI-sourced referrals for marketing content. Claude and Mistral are useful extras. More engines are only worth paying for if your audience actually uses them.
