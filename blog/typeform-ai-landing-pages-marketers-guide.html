Typeform AI Landing Pages: What's Live, What's Still Beta (2026)

Typeform added a conversational AI landing page builder to every plan this month, including free. Its own documentation and its own marketing page don't fully agree on what that includes.

BR
The BuzzRiding TeamResearched and refined with AI tools

This is a synthesis, not a hands-on test. AI Landing Pages shipped in beta on October 7, 2026, and Typeform's own help documentation warns that beta access isn't guaranteed even on a paid account: "If you don't see the option to create a page, it isn't enabled for you yet." So rather than claim a walkthrough this site can't promise a reader could reproduce, what follows is a direct read of Typeform's announcement, its documentation, and its product page — cited throughout.

📋 How this was put together

Every claim below comes from Typeform's own press announcement, its official beta documentation, and its landing page builder product page, all read on the same day in October 2026. No first-hand test of the feature is claimed anywhere in this piece.

What actually shipped on October 7

Typeform launched AI Landing Pages as a conversational builder: describe the page you want in a chat box, and Typeform AI generates a branded draft. You refine it by chatting further or editing blocks directly, then publish.

Aleks Bass, Typeform's Chief Product and Technology Officer, framed the pitch around consolidation: the tool "collapses" a stack of separately bought page builders, forms, CRMs and automation tools "into one conversation," per the press announcement.

The feature bundles four pieces. A page is built from a prompt. A form is either embedded from an existing Typeform or generated to match the page's goal. Typeform Connectors can pull context from Canva, Notion, Shopify, Slack and Stripe while the AI builds. And submissions flow automatically into Typeform Contacts.

"Free and unlimited" has real asterisks

Typeform's product page advertises a free start, and its own FAQ states the free plan allows unlimited landing pages. That part checks out plainly: the base builder isn't gated behind a paywall.

Two of the features in Typeform's own feature list are not available to everyone who signs up, though. Custom domains and payment checkout links are both marked "closed beta" on the product page itself — meaning even a paying customer may not have access yet. Built-in analytics (visitors, traffic sources, bounce rate) is listed as "coming soon," with no date attached.

One plan boundary is explicit: removing the "Powered by Typeform" badge requires a Business plan or higher, according to the help documentation. Connecting the Shopify connector for commerce context requires the same tier, per the product page. Workflow automations that route form submissions into enrichment, routing and nurture sequences need Typeform's Growth Flow tier specifically.

None of this makes the free tier fake. It does mean "free and unlimited" describes the page-and-form builder, not the full feature list Typeform promotes alongside it.

One customer number, no methodology

The product page carries a single customer quote. Florent Schmahl, a digital marketer at Shoelace, says his Typeform landing page converts at 10.6%, and that it beat his other landing pages on build speed.

That's one account's experience, presented as a testimonial rather than a measured result. Typeform doesn't say what Schmahl's other landing pages converted at, what traffic source fed the page, or over what time window the 10.6% was measured. It's a real number from a real customer, and it's also not something a reader can use to forecast their own results.

"The page says 300+ integrations in one place and 350+ in its own FAQ. Neither figure is sourced, and they're 30 words apart on the same page."

That inconsistency is small, but it's the kind of detail a press release reprint won't catch, because nobody reads the primary source twice. It doesn't change the product's usefulness. It's a reason to read a vendor's own claims with the same skepticism this site has applied to Unbounce's and Landingi's pricing pages before.

Where this fits next to the landing-page tools already reviewed here

Typeform's pitch is bundling: page, form and CRM contact in one conversational flow, with no separate embed step. BuzzRiding's Landingi review and Unbounce review both cover tools built the other way around — a dedicated page editor with AI features layered on, not a chat-first build process.

That makes the honest comparison about workflow, not features. A team already living in Typeform forms gets a landing page without learning a second editor. A team that needs fine-grained control over layout, or wants the unlimited-page tier Unbounce offers at its second pricing rung, is better served by a dedicated builder. Typeform's own page doesn't claim to replace that category. It claims to remove a step for teams who don't need it.

Worth flagging directly: nothing here compares actual page-load speed, mobile rendering, or conversion rate between Typeform's new builder and Unbounce or Landingi. That would require running real campaigns through more than one tool at once, which this piece doesn't claim to have done.

What's genuinely new versus what's a rename

Typeform has offered a basic landing page tool before; a help-center article titled "Create a landing page with Typeform Pages (beta)" is the documentation for this specific launch, and its own wording treats Pages as a current beta product, not a years-old feature getting a new coat of paint. The AI chat-to-page generation step, the Connectors context pull, and the automatic Contacts sync are what's new as of October 7.

The deeper AI groundwork — form generation from a prompt, brand-kit detection from a URL, multi-language translation — dates to Typeform's broader AI Suite rollout in early 2025, which reported more than 150,000 customers and over 600 million annual interactions at the time. AI Landing Pages is an extension of that groundwork into a new surface, not a first AI feature for the company.

What the Connectors actually do

Typeform Connectors are the other piece worth understanding before trying this. Per the product page, five are live at launch: Canva, Notion, Shopify, Slack and Stripe. Each one feeds context to Typeform AI while it builds or edits a page, rather than acting as a one-time import.

In practice, that means a Shopify-connected account can let the AI reference live product data when generating a product showcase section, and a Stripe connection is what makes the checkout-link feature possible once it leaves closed beta. Canva and Notion connectors pull brand assets and written content into the chat, so the AI has source material beyond a text prompt.

None of the five Connectors are described as optional add-ons with separate pricing; they're listed as part of the AI Landing Pages feature set. The Shopify connector is the one exception called out directly: using it requires a Business plan or higher, per the same product page, even though the other four Connectors carry no stated plan requirement.

Who should actually look at this

A solo marketer or small team already paying for Typeform forms has nothing to lose trying the free builder, once beta access is enabled on their account — it won't replace a paid landing-page tool subscription, but it costs nothing to test for a simple campaign page.

A team that needs custom-domain hosting or checkout links today, not whenever closed beta opens up, should treat those two features as unavailable for now and plan around a dedicated tool instead. A team already deep in Unbounce or Landingi has no obvious reason to switch based on what's published so far — nothing here demonstrates Typeform's builder converts better, loads faster, or is easier to use; it's simply newer and bundles more of the stack into one login.

💡 Key takeaway

Typeform's AI Landing Pages are free to build on any plan, including the free tier. Custom domains and checkout links, two of the features Typeform promotes alongside the launch, are both still closed beta. Analytics is "coming soon" with no date given. Test the builder on its current terms, not the terms its marketing page implies.

The obvious caveat

This article reads Typeform's own announcement and documentation; it is not an audit and not a hands-on test. Beta features change fast, and closed-beta gates can open at any time without updating the public product page. Confirm current access and feature availability directly in a Typeform account before planning a campaign around it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Typeform's AI Landing Pages feature free?
The base builder is free on every Typeform plan, including the free tier, with unlimited landing pages per Typeform's own FAQ. Removing the "Powered by Typeform" badge requires a Business plan or higher.
Can I use a custom domain with Typeform AI Landing Pages?
Custom domain hosting is listed on Typeform's own product page as "closed beta," meaning it isn't available to every account yet, including paying customers. The same applies to payment checkout links.
When did Typeform launch AI Landing Pages?
Typeform announced AI Landing Pages on October 7, 2026, as a conversational, prompt-to-publish landing page builder built on top of its existing AI Suite.
Does Typeform's AI Landing Pages tool include analytics?
Built-in analytics covering visitors, traffic sources and bounce rate is listed as "coming soon" on Typeform's product page, with no release date given as of this writing.
Should I switch from Unbounce or Landingi to Typeform for landing pages?
Nothing published so far compares conversion rate, load speed or usability between them. Typeform's advantage is bundling a page, form and CRM contact in one flow; dedicated builders like Unbounce and Landingi still offer more layout control and, on Unbounce, an earlier unlimited-page tier.
