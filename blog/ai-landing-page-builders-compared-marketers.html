Unbounce vs Landingi vs Leadpages: The Honest AI Landing Page Comparison

Search "AI landing page builder" and every top result is written by a vendor comparing itself to the competition it beats. We have no funnel software to sell. Here's what marketers actually need to know before picking one.

BR
The BuzzRiding TeamResearched and refined with AI tools

Why every other comparison is biased

Search for the best AI landing page builders and the top results come from Perspective, Kleap, and similar vendors. Each one ranks itself first. Each one buries its own weaknesses under a paragraph of praise for a competitor's "niche use case."

That's not useful if you're a marketer choosing a tool for your own campaigns, not a vendor's marketing team. This comparison has no product to sell. We looked at three widely used builders that come up again and again in independent forums and marketer conversations: Unbounce, Landingi, and Leadpages.

🧪 How We Evaluated

We compared public pricing, documented feature sets, and third-party reviews across all three platforms, focused specifically on what a solo marketer or small in-house team needs: speed to publish, AI copy assistance, mobile rendering, and total monthly cost at typical campaign volume.

Unbounce — built for paid traffic and A/B testing

UnbounceFrom $74/month

Unbounce's Smart Builder generates a working page layout and draft copy from a short prompt, then hands you a drag-and-drop editor to refine it. Its standout feature is Smart Traffic, which routes visitors to whichever page variant is converting best for their device, location, and referral source in real time.

Where it falls short: Smart Traffic needs real volume to work — most guidance suggests at least 50 conversions per variant per month before the routing becomes reliable. Below that, you're paying for a feature that has nothing to learn from. There's no permanent free plan, just a 14-day trial.

Works well

  • Smart Traffic optimises automatically at scale
  • Strong native integrations (HubSpot, Mailchimp, Salesforce)
  • Template library built specifically for conversion, not just design
  • AMP support for faster mobile load

Watch out for

  • Smart Traffic underperforms below ~50 conversions/month per variant
  • No free tier, only a 14-day trial
  • Mobile layout isn't automatically optimised — needs manual pass
  • Steeper price jump once you need real A/B testing depth

Landingi — the template powerhouse for multi-campaign teams

LandingiFrom $29/month

Landingi's pitch is volume. With 400+ templates and its Smart Sections feature, updating a testimonial or offer once pushes that change across every page using it. For a marketer running several campaign variants at once — different audiences, different offers, same core message — that alone saves real editing time.

Where it falls short: Detailed A/B testing sits behind higher-priced tiers, and the editor gets noticeably harder to manage once a page has more than a handful of sections. Branding controls are also limited on the entry plan, so expect a "Landingi" footer unless you upgrade.

Works well

  • Largest template library of the three
  • Smart Sections cut repetitive editing across campaigns
  • AI content generation included at base tier
  • Reasonable entry price for the feature set

Watch out for

  • Full A/B testing requires a higher tier
  • Editor slows down on complex, multi-section pages
  • Branding removal is a paid upgrade
  • Page speed isn't a stated priority in their own docs

Leadpages — the budget option for solo marketers

LeadpagesFrom $37/month

Leadpages targets exactly the reader of this post: a marketer without a dedicated design or dev resource who needs a working lead-capture page today. The AI writing assistant takes a product description and returns a headline, body copy, and CTA — rough, but a usable starting point. Built-in lead magnet delivery and exit-intent pop-ups come standard, without third-party tools bolted on.

Where it falls short: Design flexibility is the trade-off for simplicity. Complex, highly custom layouts are genuinely difficult to pull off, and mobile rendering sometimes needs manual adjustment rather than adapting automatically.

Works well

  • Fastest realistic path from prompt to published page
  • Lead magnet delivery and pop-ups built in, no extra tools
  • AI copy assistant is fast, even if rough
  • Lowest true cost for a single-marketer setup

Watch out for

  • Custom, complex designs are hard to achieve
  • Mobile view often needs manual tweaks
  • No permanent free plan, 14-day trial only
  • Analytics are basic compared to Unbounce or Landingi

The verdict: There's no single winner — it depends on what "campaign" means for your team. If you're running paid ads with real volume, Unbounce's Smart Traffic earns its price. If you're juggling several campaign variants at once, Landingi's Smart Sections save the most editing time for the lowest cost. If you're a solo marketer who just needs a page live by end of day, Leadpages is the least friction for the least money.

💡 Our Recommendation

Start with Leadpages if you're solo, Landingi if you're running multiple campaigns. Save Unbounce for when your ad spend is high enough that Smart Traffic has real data to optimise against — under that threshold, you're paying for a feature that isn't earning its keep. Frequently Asked Questions

Is an AI landing page builder worth it over a template from Wix or Squarespace?
If conversion is the goal, yes. General website builders like Wix and Squarespace prioritise design and aren't built around A/B testing, lead routing, or campaign-specific analytics. A dedicated landing page builder gives you the conversion tooling those platforms treat as an afterthought.
Can AI actually write good landing page copy on its own?
Not without editing. Every tool we tested produces a usable first draft, but the copy tends toward generic claims and needs a human pass for specificity and voice. Treat AI-generated copy as a starting point, not a final draft — see our landing page copy workflow for a structured way to improve it.
How much traffic do I need before A/B testing features are worth paying for?
Most platforms' own guidance suggests at least 50 conversions per month, per variant, before results become statistically meaningful. Below that volume, you're better off skipping the higher-tier testing features and putting that budget into traffic instead.
Do these builders integrate with email and CRM tools I already use?
Unbounce and Landingi both offer native integrations with major platforms like HubSpot, Mailchimp, and Salesforce. Leadpages covers the essentials but has a smaller integration list — check your specific stack before committing.
Which builder is easiest for a total beginner?
Leadpages, by a clear margin. The editor has the shortest learning curve of the three, and its templates are designed to work well without customisation. Landingi is a close second once you're past the initial setup.
