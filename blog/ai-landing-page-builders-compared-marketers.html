Unbounce vs Landingi vs Leadpages: Pricing Compared (September 2026)

This is a synthesis, not a hands-on test. What follows is what each vendor's own pricing page states today — dated, sourced, and updated after Leadpages rebuilt its entire platform this year.

BR
The BuzzRiding TeamResearched and refined with AI tools

Why this is a pricing comparison, not a hands-on test

An earlier version of this article described a hands-on comparison of Unbounce, Landingi and Leadpages that never happened. Running a real side-by-side would mean live campaigns on three paid platforms at once — a budget this outlet doesn't have. Rather than delete the topic, this is the honest replacement: what each vendor's own pricing page states, read directly, dated, and cited.

The timing matters. Leadpages rebuilt its entire platform in 2026, splitting into a publishing-only tier (HTML Pub) and a full conversion-optimization product (Leadpages proper) — a bigger structural change than either competitor made this year.

📋 How this was put together

Every price and plan limit below is pulled from the vendor's own site — unbounce.com/compare-plan-features, landingi.com/pricing and leadpages.com/pricing — as published in September 2026. No first-hand testing is claimed anywhere in this piece. SaaS pricing changes without notice, so confirm with the vendor before you buy.

Unbounce — five tiers, A/B testing gated behind the third one

UnbounceFrom $22/mo (annual)

Unbounce's current ladder, per its own comparison page: Starter at $29/month ($22 annually), Build at $99 ($74 annually), Experiment at $149 ($112 annually), Optimize at $249 ($187 annually), plus custom-priced Concierge and Agency tiers.

What the tiers actually gate: Starter caps at 500 monthly visitors, 1 landing page limit of 5, and 1 user — before you can even A/B test. A/B testing itself doesn't unlock until the $149 Experiment tier. Smart Traffic, Unbounce's AI visitor-routing feature, requires the $249 Optimize tier. Visitor limits scale from 500 (Starter) to 20,000 (Build), 30,000 (Experiment), 50,000 (Optimize), and 100,000+ on Concierge.

Per the vendor's page

  • Unlimited landing pages from the Build tier up
  • Smart Traffic AI routing at the Optimize tier
  • Audit logs and a dedicated success manager on Concierge/Agency
  • 25% discount for annual billing across every fixed tier

Worth checking before buying

  • A/B testing is not available below the $149/month tier
  • Starter tier caps at 500 visitors and 1 user
  • Smart Traffic is gated behind the $249/month tier
  • No permanent free plan

Landingi — credits-based AI usage, unlimited seats on the entry tier

LandingiFrom $24/mo (annual)

Landingi's own pricing page lists four tiers: Build at $24/month annually (10 pages, 2,000 visits/month, 1 domain, 2,500 AI credits), Optimize at $119/month annually (100 pages, 30,000 visits, 3 domains, 10,000 credits, server-side A/B testing, Solis AI Insights), Scale at $229+/month annually (unlimited pages, 100,000–500,000 visits, Orbit MCP server), and Enterprise from $1,199/month (1M+ visits, SSO, dedicated account manager).

What's distinctive: every Landingi tier, including the cheapest, includes its AI page generator ("Lunar") — usage is metered by credits rather than gated by tier. A/B testing is server-side but doesn't appear until the $119 Optimize tier. Visits and AI credits, not seats, are the practical limiter on the entry plan.

Per the vendor's page

  • AI page generation (Lunar) included on every tier, even the cheapest
  • Unlimited conversions on all tiers
  • Solis AI Insights (automated recommendations) from Optimize up
  • Two months free on annual billing

Worth checking before buying

  • Build tier caps at 2,000 visits/month — low for active campaigns
  • A/B testing requires the $119/month Optimize tier
  • AI features are usage-capped by credits, not unlimited
  • Extra domains cost $5/month past the plan's included count

Leadpages — rebuilt in 2026, testing bundled in from $99

LeadpagesFrom $99/mo (or $10 for publishing only)

Leadpages restructured its entire product in 2026 into two lines. HTML Pub is publishing-only — Starter $10/month, Pro $29/month, Business $49/month — pages, domains and an AI editor, no A/B testing. Leadpages proper adds conversion-rate-optimization tooling: Grow $99/month, Optimize $199/month, Scale $399/month.

What changed: per the vendor's own pricing page, every Leadpages-proper tier now includes unlimited traffic and unlimited pages — no visitor cap on any plan, which the page explicitly contrasts with Unbounce's 20,000-visitor cap at the equivalent $99 price point. Manual A/B testing and dynamic text replacement are included starting on Grow ($99); Smart Traffic (AI routing) and heatmaps require Optimize ($199) and up.

Per the vendor's page

  • No traffic cap on any Leadpages-proper tier
  • A/B testing bundled in from the $99 Grow tier, not gated to a higher one
  • Lead enrichment and dynamic text replacement included from Grow up
  • Publishing-only HTML Pub option from $10/month for teams not ready for CRO

Worth checking before buying

  • No landing-page tier under $99/month with testing included
  • Smart Traffic and heatmaps require the $199 Optimize tier
  • 7-day trial, shorter than Unbounce's and Landingi's 14 days
  • Full "auto-optimization loop" (continuous testing) is Scale-only at $399

💡 What this data doesn't answer

None of this is a verdict on output quality, page-load speed, or which tool's A/B testing actually moves conversion rates further. That would require running campaigns through all three and comparing results — exactly the test the earlier version of this article claimed to have run and hadn't. Frequently Asked Questions

Which is cheapest: Unbounce, Landingi or Leadpages?
On entry paid tiers, Landingi's Build plan is cheapest at $24/month billed annually ($29 monthly). Unbounce's Starter plan is $22/month annually ($29 monthly) but caps at 500 visitors and 1 user, which most marketers will outgrow immediately. Leadpages no longer sells a true entry landing-page tier below $99/month (Grow) now that A/B testing is bundled in from that tier up.
Does Leadpages still have an entry-level plan like it used to?
Not in the same shape. As of September 2026, Leadpages split into two product lines: HTML Pub (publishing only, from $10/month) and Leadpages proper (CRO with A/B testing, from $99/month for the Grow plan). The old single mid-priced plan with basic testing no longer exists — testing now requires the $99 tier.
Which of the three includes A/B testing at the lowest price?
Leadpages, per its own pricing page: manual A/B testing is included starting on the $99/month Grow plan. Unbounce requires its $149/month Experiment plan for A/B testing. Landingi requires its $119/month Optimize plan for server-side A/B testing.
Do these builders integrate with email and CRM tools?
Yes, on all three. Leadpages' pricing page lists HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp, Google Ads, Stripe, Shopify, Slack and Zapier among 200+ integrations. Landingi and Unbounce both list native integrations with major CRM and email platforms alongside Zapier support; check each vendor's integrations page for your specific stack before committing.
Which builder has the highest visitor or traffic caps?
Leadpages, by removing the concept entirely: its pricing page states unlimited traffic on every plan, including the $99 Grow tier. Unbounce and Landingi both meter visits by tier — Unbounce from 500 (Starter) up to 100,000+ (Concierge), Landingi from 2,000 (Build) up to 1M+ (Enterprise).
