Supermetrics Gave Claude Write Access to 6 Ad Platforms. Here's What's Missing From Every Writeup

On 3 November 2026, Supermetrics extended its Claude connector from reporting into campaign management. Claude can now create and edit live campaigns across six ad platforms, paused until a human approves them.

BR
The BuzzRiding TeamResearched and refined with AI tools

This is a synthesis of Supermetrics' own announcement, its product page, and independent coverage — not a hands-on test. No BuzzRiding account was connected to Supermetrics, Claude or any ad platform to run this first-hand. What follows is sourced and cited throughout, with every vendor claim flagged as a vendor claim.

Supermetrics has offered a Claude connector for marketing reporting since February 2026. On 3 November, per ppc.land's coverage of the announcement, that connector grew a second job: writing. Marketers can now ask Claude to create or edit campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok, LinkedIn and Snapchat, directly from a chat.

That is a bigger step than a reporting update. A wrong number in a report is an annoyance. A wrong action on a live ad account spends real money. The announcement leans hard on one safeguard to cover that gap: every new campaign is created paused, and stays paused until a person approves it.

📋 How this was put together

Built from Supermetrics' own Claude product page, ppc.land's announcement writeup, MarketScale's coverage, and Anthropic's own published customer story. Every figure below is attributed to whichever of those sources stated it; none is this site's own measurement.

What the approval gate actually does

Per Supermetrics co-CEO Mikael Thuneberg, quoted by MarketScale: "Campaigns get created in a paused state, not live, so nothing goes to market without a human actively choosing." He added that the system "should never take an action that could surprise the user."

In practice, per Supermetrics' own product page, that means three things. New campaigns launch paused. Proposed edits to existing, already-live campaigns can be reviewed before they take effect. And customers can set stricter approval requirements on some accounts than others — useful for an agency running several clients through one workspace.

Anthropic's customer story names one such agency. Layer, a 25-person shop in Norway, used to quote clients 10 hours of billable work for a report pulling from five data sources. With the connector, Anthropic reports that work now takes 20 minutes, 17 of which go to placing the finished data in a client's Google Sheet. Layer strategist Morten Kleven spent weeks validating Claude's output before rolling it out, and is quoted saying: "I have not found a single hallucination or incorrect data point."

That figure describes reporting, the older half of the connector, not the new campaign-write feature. Anthropic's story doesn't report a validation period for campaign creation specifically. Worth noting as read, not as this site's own finding either way.

"The system should never take an action that could surprise the user." — Mikael Thuneberg, Supermetrics co-CEO, on the approval gate that's doing all the safety work here.

What's missing from every writeup, including Supermetrics' own

ppc.land's coverage is the most detailed independent account of the launch, and it's explicit about what the announcement leaves out. Four gaps stand out for a marketer deciding whether to turn this on.

  • Pricing and plan tier aren't stated. Neither Supermetrics' product page nor the announcement coverage says which Claude plan is required, or which Supermetrics subscription includes campaign write access. A 14-day free trial is offered, which is the only pricing detail published anywhere reviewed for this piece.
  • Platform parity isn't confirmed. The six-platform list is consistent across sources, but no source states whether every platform supports the same set of write actions — budget changes, bid changes, and ad-group-level edits aren't itemized per platform.
  • Nobody says whether approval can be switched off. Supermetrics frames per-account approval levels as a feature, letting stricter review apply to some clients than others. That implies a looser setting exists somewhere in the range. Whether "looser" ever means "skip review entirely" isn't addressed in any source reviewed here.
  • Security isn't addressed. ppc.land flags that write-capable chat connectors carry tool-poisoning and prompt-injection risk in general. The Supermetrics announcement doesn't engage with either concern for this specific feature.

One more data point worth holding onto: per ppc.land, Google's own MCP connector for its ad platform was read-only as of October 2025. If that hasn't changed, Supermetrics' write path into Google Ads runs through a third party's integration, not Google's own, for at least one of the six platforms.

This is the fourth write-access launch this fall, not the first

Supermetrics isn't the first marketing tool to hand Claude a pen instead of just a read-only dashboard. BuzzRiding has covered three earlier moves in the same direction this fall, and setting them side by side shows a pattern none of the individual announcements name.

Constant Contact's Claude connector, live from 10 September, lets Claude draft and publish email and social campaigns, with a one-click send back into an account you already own. HubSpot's Agent Hub picked up write access to CRM records back in November 2025, ahead of this fall's Agent Hub and Agent Builder expansion. Adobe added Acrobat to its Claude plugin on 24 September, on top of Photoshop and Express access it already had.

Supermetrics is the first of the four where the thing being written isn't content or a CRM record — it's ad spend. That's a meaningfully different risk category, and it's the reason the approval gate carries more weight here than in any of the earlier three launches.

Across all four, one question stays unanswered everywhere: what happens when more than one person on a team can approve changes. None of the four vendors' public documentation, reviewed for this piece or the three earlier ones, describes multi-user approval permissions. For a solo marketer, that's a non-issue. For an agency running shared accounts, like Layer, it's the detail that decides whether this is safe to turn on for more than one person at a time.

Adoption numbers, with the caveats attached

ppc.land reports that more than 6,000 customers have used Supermetrics' Claude connector since its February 2026 launch, with active users growing by an average of 250% month over month. Supermetrics says it serves more than 200,000 businesses in total, which puts 6,000 at roughly 3% of that base. No starting count or definition of "active user" accompanies the growth figure, and ppc.land flags both as unaudited vendor claims.

Worth repeating: those adoption numbers describe the connector overall, mostly accrued during its reporting-only phase. They say nothing about how many customers have tried the new campaign-write feature specifically, since it is six days old at publication.

💡 Key takeaway

The approval gate is a real, specific safeguard: new campaigns launch paused, not live. But pricing, plan requirements, platform-by-platform action parity, and whether approval can be turned off are all missing from the announcement and the coverage of it. Ask Supermetrics those four questions directly before connecting a live ad account.

Who should look at this now

A team already paying for both Supermetrics and a Claude plan has the least friction to pilot this. Start with the edit path on a single paused or low-budget campaign, not a live one, and confirm in writing what your plan tier actually includes before scaling up. An agency managing several client accounts should ask Supermetrics directly about multi-user approval before letting more than one person connect.

Anyone without an existing Supermetrics account has less reason to buy in specifically for this. The write feature is an extension of a reporting tool you'd need to adopt anyway; it isn't a standalone product.

The obvious caveat

This piece is a reading of public announcements and documentation, not an audit of the product. Supermetrics may answer the four open questions above directly if asked; none of it is published where this research could find it. Treat every figure above as "Supermetrics says" or "this outlet reported," and confirm directly inside your own account before connecting it to a live ad budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

What did Supermetrics actually announce?
On 3 November 2026, Supermetrics extended its Claude connector to campaign management. Marketers can now create and edit campaigns on six ad platforms from a Claude chat. New campaigns are created paused, until a person approves them.
Can Claude spend my ad budget without my approval?
Per Supermetrics' own product page and its co-CEO's statements, new campaigns are created paused, not live, and stay that way until a person approves them. Whether approval can be disabled for an account isn't addressed in any source reviewed for this piece.
How much does the Supermetrics Claude connector cost?
Not published. Supermetrics' product page offers a 14-day free trial but doesn't state which subscription tier includes campaign write access, or whether it carries a separate charge from the existing reporting connector.
Is this the first marketing tool to give Claude write access?
No. HubSpot added Claude write access to CRM records in November 2025, Constant Contact launched a campaign-drafting connector on 10 September 2026, and Adobe added Acrobat to its existing Claude plugin on 24 September 2026. Supermetrics is the first of these where the write action touches live ad spend rather than content or CRM records.
Does every supported ad platform get the same features?
Not confirmed. Supermetrics lists six platforms — Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok, LinkedIn and Snapchat — but no source reviewed for this piece states whether budget, bid and ad-group-level edits are available identically across all six.
