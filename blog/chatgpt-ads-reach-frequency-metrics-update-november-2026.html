ChatGPT Ads Just Got 9 New Metrics in One Quiet Email. Here's What Actually Changed.

On 25 November 2026, OpenAI emailed ChatGPT Ads advertisers five product changes, led by nine new audience metrics. Two trade outlets covered it within a day of each other, and quietly disagree on a detail OpenAI itself hasn't clarified.

BR
The BuzzRiding TeamResearched and refined with AI tools

ChatGPT Ads got its last big public upgrade on 5 October 2026: a visual ad format and a long roster of measurement partners. That one came with a press release. The next one didn't.

On 25 November 2026, OpenAI sent a product-update email straight to advertisers, titled "ChatGPT Ads Product Update." No blog post, no press push. PPC Land and Relevant Audience picked it up within a day of each other, each citing the same email. It's a smaller story than October's launch, but it matters more to anyone already spending money on the platform.

🔬 How this was reviewed

This piece is built from two independent reports of the same 25 November OpenAI advertiser email, plus wider reporting on ChatGPT's ad business. No BuzzRiding account advertises on ChatGPT, so nothing here is presented as something tested first-hand. Every figure is attributed to OpenAI or the outlet that reported it, and we flag the one point where our two primary sources disagree.

The nine metrics, and what OpenAI actually said about them

Per PPC Land's report of the email, Ads Manager and the Ads API now surface nine audience-level metrics. Two are headline numbers: Reach and Frequency.

OpenAI's own description, quoted by PPC Land: Reach "estimates how many unique people saw your ads at least once." Frequency is total impressions divided by Reach. That's the same formula advertisers already know from Meta and Google.

The other seven are named in the email but not defined: Click Reach, Click Frequency, Total Reach, Total Frequency, Unique CTR, Unique CPC, and Cost per 1,000 Reached. Their names are self-explanatory by convention, but OpenAI did not publish formulas for them, so treat the exact math as unconfirmed until OpenAI's help documentation catches up.

This is an addition, not a replacement. Per PPC Land, reporting before this update covered impressions, clicks, spend, CTR, CPC, CPM, and conversions, with view-through conversions added back in August 2026.

Business Agents move outside the US, with real limits

The second headline change: Business Agents, OpenAI's tool for letting a business run its own presence inside ChatGPT, are now open to "eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in supported markets outside the U.S.," per the email as quoted by PPC Land. Neither outlet reports OpenAI naming which markets qualify.

Business Agents only went live in the US on 10 November 2026, per PPC Land's timeline. This expansion arrives just 15 days later, which is a fast international push by any platform's standard.

A third, separate item: a Sponsored Business Agent, available to advertisers who already have Business Agent access. Per OpenAI's help documentation, it's rolling out "over the coming weeks" and requires the Conversion objective to be enabled.

Three smaller changes worth knowing

The email also covered three lower-profile updates, per PPC Land's report:

  • A "Learn more" link now appears on single-image ads by default, giving users an extra click-through path.
  • A "Hide product sale prices" setting is now available under Product display, for advertisers running product-feed ad groups.
  • More detail on rejected-ad policy, though OpenAI says its underlying approval guidelines have not changed.

The detail two outlets can't agree on

Here's where the published coverage gets genuinely useful to check before you act on it. PPC Land and Relevant Audience both covered the same email, within a day of each other, and landed on different facts about one thing: whether OpenAI quietly dropped the "US-only" wording from its own help page.

PPC Land reports that, as of the evening of 25 November, OpenAI's "Setting up your business on ChatGPT" help page still described Business Agents as US-only. Relevant Audience reports that the same help page was edited on 26 November to remove that wording.

Neither outlet is wrong, necessarily. A help page edited between two outlets' fact-checks would explain both reports perfectly. But it also means the "outside the U.S." expansion described in the headline change above may still be in a transitional, half-documented state. If you're evaluating Business Agents for a non-US business right now, check OpenAI's live help page yourself rather than trusting either article's snapshot.

Relevant Audience adds one more wrinkle worth knowing. Thailand appears as a self-service market on OpenAI's own Ads Manager availability page, with ads reportedly rolling out there since 23 September 2026. But no OpenAI statement names Thailand as a Business Agents market specifically, and self-service ad buying isn't the same product as Business Agent access.

A third, weaker signal: an APAC partner story

A separate, same-week item is worth noting with more caution. A 27 November report describes Adform as a new technology partner for ChatGPT Ads across the Asia-Pacific region, putting ChatGPT ads live in "over 50 markets," including Australia, Singapore, Japan, and Thailand.

We're flagging this one differently than the first two sources. The outlet itself states it incorporates reporting from B&T, and its market-count figure doesn't obviously reconcile with other reporting on ChatGPT's international reach. Treat the Adform partnership as directionally real, but verify the specific market list against Adform's or OpenAI's own statements before repeating the "50 markets" figure anywhere that matters.

Why this keeps happening with ChatGPT Ads specifically

None of this is happening in a vacuum. eMarketer has reported that ChatGPT is signing up advertisers faster than OpenAI can prove the ads work. That pattern fits a platform still building its measurement and documentation layer in public, one email at a time, instead of shipping a finished ad product on day one.

That's a reasonable way to build an ad platform. It's a harder way to be a marketer trying to budget against it, since the authoritative source of truth (a help page, in this case) can change underneath a story that's already been published and shared internally at your company.

What this means if you're running or considering ChatGPT Ads

Four practical takeaways, based strictly on what's published above:

  • Pull your Reach and Frequency numbers once they appear in Ads Manager, and compare them to your Meta and Google reach figures for the same period. A platform-reported Reach number is only useful once you've sanity-checked it against a number you already trust.
  • Don't assume the other seven metrics match your existing definitions. OpenAI named them but hasn't published formulas. Ask your OpenAI rep directly, or wait for the help documentation to update, before building them into a report you'll show a client.
  • If you're outside the US, check the live help page before budgeting. The US-only wording may or may not still be there depending on exactly when you look.
  • Don't repeat the "50 markets" APAC figure without a second source. It comes from the weakest of the three reports reviewed here.

💡 Key takeaway

OpenAI added real reporting depth to ChatGPT Ads on 25 November, through an advertiser email rather than a public announcement. The two outlets that covered it agree on the five changes, but disagree on whether the platform's own help page still says Business Agents are US-only. Until OpenAI publishes something more authoritative than an email, check the live help page yourself before you plan around any of this.

Frequently Asked Questions

What did OpenAI actually change in ChatGPT Ads on 25 November 2026?
Per an advertiser email reported by PPC Land, OpenAI made five changes: nine new audience metrics led by Reach and Frequency, Business Agents expanding to markets outside the US, a Sponsored Business Agent rolling out, a default "Learn more" link on single-image ads, and a new setting to hide product sale prices.
What do Reach and Frequency mean in ChatGPT Ads?
Per OpenAI's own wording, Reach estimates how many unique people saw an ad at least once. Frequency is total impressions divided by Reach. The other seven new metrics (Click Reach, Click Frequency, Total Reach, Total Frequency, Unique CTR, Unique CPC, and Cost per 1,000 Reached) were named but not formally defined in the email.
Are Business Agents now available outside the United States?
OpenAI's email says Business Agents are open to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in supported markets outside the US, without naming those markets. PPC Land and Relevant Audience disagree on whether OpenAI's help page still describes the feature as US-only, so check the live page before assuming availability in your market.
Is ChatGPT Ads reporting now as mature as Google Ads or Meta?
Not based on what's published here. eMarketer has reported that ChatGPT is gaining advertisers faster than OpenAI can prove ad performance, and the fact that this update arrived by advertiser email rather than published documentation suggests the reporting layer is still being finalized.
Where can I read OpenAI's original announcement?
This update was sent directly to advertisers by email rather than published as a blog post, so there is no OpenAI announcement page to link to. The two primary reports reviewed here are from PPC Land and Relevant Audience, both published 26 November 2026.
