Landingi Review 2026: Pricing, Lunar AI, and Is It Worth It?

Most Landingi reviews online come from affiliate sites chasing a commission. This one doesn't have a dog in that fight. Here's what Landingi actually costs, what its new AI generator can do, and who should skip it.

What Landingi actually is

Landingi is a landing page builder aimed at marketers who run several campaigns at once. It sits between Leadpages and Unbounce: a pixel-perfect drag-and-drop editor like Unbounce, priced closer to Leadpages.

The pitch is volume without chaos. Over 400 templates, a Smart Sections feature that updates one element across many pages, and — new this year — Lunar, an AI page generator built into the platform.

🧪 How We Evaluated

We checked Landingi's current published pricing, tested how Lunar generates a page from a prompt, and cross-referenced independent user reviews on G2 and Capterra against the vendor's own claims.

Landingi pricing, plan by plan

LandingiFrom $29/month

Landingi runs four paid tiers: Build, Optimize, Scale, and Enterprise. Build starts at $29 a month, or $24 a month billed annually.

Build ($29/mo): 10 landing pages, 2,000 monthly visits, one custom domain. Fine for a single ongoing campaign.

Optimize and Scale: Higher visit caps, more domains, and full A/B testing unlock at these tiers. Pricing climbs toward the low hundreds per month depending on traffic volume.

Enterprise: Custom pricing for agencies and larger teams running many client accounts at once.

Most plans include unlimited conversions, so a campaign that unexpectedly goes viral won't spike your bill the way visitor-capped tools can.

Lunar: the new AI page generator

Lunar AIIncluded

Lunar takes a short description of your campaign, offer, and audience and returns a complete page: layout, copy, and a lead capture form, ready to edit.

You can refine sections directly in a chat interface rather than jumping into a separate builder. A full visual editor for Lunar output is still rolling out.

Where it falls short: Lunar speeds up the first draft, but mobile rendering still needs a manual pass. Expect 30 to 60 minutes from prompt to a page you'd actually publish.

Works well

  • Full page from a plain-language prompt
  • Editing happens without leaving the platform
  • Good starting point for non-designers
  • Cuts first-draft time significantly

Watch out for

  • Mobile layout needs manual review
  • Full visual editor not yet complete
  • Copy still reads generic without a human pass

Templates and Smart Sections

Landingi's template library is the largest of the major builders — useful if you're running several campaign variants with the same core offer.

Smart Sections let you update one element, like a testimonial or pricing block, and push that change across every page using it. For a marketer managing five landing pages for one launch, that alone saves real editing time.

Where it falls short: The editor gets noticeably slower once a page has many sections. Branding removal and full A/B testing depth sit behind higher tiers, not the entry plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Landingi good for beginners?
It's more approachable than Unbounce but has a steeper curve than Leadpages. Lunar's prompt-based generation helps beginners get a working first page fast, though the pixel-perfect editor still takes time to learn.
How much does Landingi actually cost per month?
Plans start at $29/month ($24/month billed annually) for the Build tier, covering 10 pages and 2,000 monthly visits. Optimize and Scale tiers cost more and add higher traffic caps and full A/B testing.
Is Landingi better than Unbounce?
Depends on your use case. Landingi is cheaper and better for managing many campaign variants at once. Unbounce has deeper A/B testing and Smart Traffic, which suits high-volume paid ad teams better.
What is Lunar and is it worth using?
Lunar is Landingi's built-in AI page generator. It's worth using for a fast first draft, but treat the output as a starting point — mobile layout and copy specificity still need a manual pass.
Does Landingi have a free plan?
No permanent free plan, but there's a 14-day free trial. That's enough time to build and test a real campaign page before deciding.
